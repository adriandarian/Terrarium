Brambit - fidelity remains open

Brambit is now a physical Blender source with a stepped body, tan face and belly, charcoal eyes and nose, four short legs and three asymmetrical leafy shoots. Five closed union volumes remove coplanar overlap artifacts, while 240 original construction blocks remain hidden and editable in the source file. The model exports to 24,640 triangles with one opaque, nonmetallic material and three packed PNG8 maps. The normal Blender project reopened independently. It is saved in HomesteadBlender at 76.70 cm tall, on courtyard grass near the existing explorer. Twenty imported sole samples across four feet match the source and clear the uneven ground by at most 4.31 mm after map reload. This is an initial physical model with substantial fidelity work remaining.

  • The body remains more rectangular and regularly tiled than the reference; the cap needs more asymmetric heights and the brown forehead peak needs a broader transition into the moss.
  • Bark size, depth and distribution still differ from the source. The rear tier is too flat and is inferred from the visible body rather than supplied rear artwork.
  • The main tan face follows measured source boundaries, but the lower belly steps, foot proportions and spacing need closer matching.
  • The three leaf silhouettes are recognizable, but their block seams, stem exposure and depth arrangement remain different. Small creases remain on oblique leaf surfaces.
  • The current pigment maps are simple flat colors. Surface variation and source lighting are not matched; the Unreal face is darker than the studio reference.
  • Four feet and twenty sole samples pass static support checks, with uneven-ground clearance below 5.5 mm. This model has no rig, animation, locomotion or physics validation.
