Storm - fidelity remains open

Storm R4 has 37 editable closed parts and 5,072 exported triangles. The crest was moved in depth, cloud clusters were attached and two missing lower cloud blocks were added. Its 27 body solids form one connected group with 34 verified interior overlap witnesses; ten source sparks remain separate. Three packed PNG8 maps feed two opaque nonmetallic material roles. The normal Blender file reopened independently. The 15.17 cm display is saved beside Grove; three tip-contact samples and twelve post-face clearance samples passed after reload. Substantial shape and surface fidelity work remains.

  • The main bolt is a closed faceted extrusion, but its gold perimeter and cream ridge differ from the concept in width, bends and relief.
  • The 27 non-spark solids now form one connected group with positive interior overlap witnesses. They remain editable overlapping meshes, not a Boolean-unioned manifold.
  • Cloud cluster sizes, spacing and partial overlaps still differ from the source.
  • The removal of arbitrary triangular creases makes the caps cleaner, but the main gold body and cream ridge are now too flat. Deliberate stepped volumes and source-matched facets are still needed; the back remains slab-like.
  • Ten sparks follow the concept arrangement, with small remaining position and size differences.
  • Static tip contact and sampled post clearance passed; rigid-body balance, animation, gameplay and full collision have not been tested.
