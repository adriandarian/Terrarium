Storm - fidelity remains open

Storm R11 has 50 editable closed parts and 6,640 exported triangles. The upper backing sheet was removed and replaced by complete stepped gold blocks; four sculpted solids form the diagonal gold edge. The crest and upper clouds were rejoined to this volume. Its 40 body solids form one connected group with 60 verified interior overlap witnesses; ten source sparks remain separate. Three packed PNG8 maps feed two opaque nonmetallic material roles. The normal Blender file reopened independently. The 15.42 cm display is saved beside Grove; three tip-contact samples and twelve post-face clearance samples passed after reload. Substantial shape and surface fidelity work remains.

  • The stepped gold upper body and sculpted gold diagonal improve the volume, but their outlines, proportions and facet boundaries still differ from the concept.
  • The 40 non-spark solids now form one connected group with positive interior overlap witnesses. They remain editable overlapping meshes, not a Boolean-unioned manifold.
  • Cloud cluster sizes, spacing and partial overlaps still differ from the source.
  • The cream ridge still reads as a continuous flat strip: the source has separate raised cream blocks, a wider crossbar top and lower angled facets. The lower rear core remains slab-like and needs a coherent volumetric reconstruction.
  • Ten sparks follow the concept arrangement, with small remaining position and size differences.
  • Static tip contact and sampled post clearance passed; rigid-body balance, animation, gameplay and full collision have not been tested.
