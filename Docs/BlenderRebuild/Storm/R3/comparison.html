Storm - fidelity remains open

Storm has 35 editable closed parts and 4,872 exported triangles: a gold lightning body, projecting cream ridge, crest and cloud blocks, and ten sparks. Three packed PNG8 maps feed two opaque nonmetallic material roles. The normal Blender file reopened independently. The 16.10 cm display is saved beside Grove; three tip-contact samples and twelve post-face clearance samples passed after reload. This is an initial reconstruction with substantial fidelity work remaining.

  • The main bolt is a closed faceted extrusion, but its gold perimeter and cream ridge differ from the concept in width, bends and relief.
  • Crest cubes and cloud blocks are separate closed parts. Several remain visibly detached from the main bolt in side/rear views; connected physical construction is not established.
  • Cloud cluster sizes, spacing and partial overlaps still differ from the source.
  • Back surfaces and thickness are inferred from a single view; the rear currently reads as a broad faceted slab.
  • Ten sparks follow the concept arrangement, with small remaining position and size differences.
  • Static tip contact and sampled post clearance passed; rigid-body balance, animation, gameplay and full collision have not been tested.
