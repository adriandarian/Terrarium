## Focused remaining work

1. Ground and vegetation (7, 9, feeding 1): highest image-area impact. The
   foreground needs composed patches, not an even scattering of more plants.
2. Cliff material/edge integration (4, 6, feeding 1 and 13): visibly interrupt
   masonry courses and flat green lids in the cottage-adjacent and river cliffs.
3. Shoreline (13): remove the floating-square visual read of the pale shallow
   stones and thicken selected grounded plant pockets. Existing new terrace
   topology is useful and should be retained.
4. Depth and bridge (3, 17): make the upper-background attenuation visible at
   the final viewing scale and reduce bridge support heaviness.

The scene has made substantial measurable visual progress. It still cannot
honestly be reported as all 18 resolved from this render, chiefly because the
reference's ground/plant/cliff integration remains absent across large areas.
