# Deep Delver Mark - revision 12

The front was rebuilt after the user rejected R8's disconnected posts, layering
and colors. Four nested V-shaped block courses now descend into the central well.
Short diamond-topped blocks sit on solid stepped risers, with a charcoal outer
arch and a second teal arch behind it. The palette combines gray teal, slate,
deep blue green and pale crystal colors. The central crystal arrangement uses
landmarks from the 1254px original, with its upper peak lowered enough to clear
the existing front mount.

This is real modeled relief: closed beveled prisms, stepped extruded walls and
separate physical depth levels. No reference image was painted over the render.
The front key light was moved to screen left and the fill adjusted to expose the
two block facets. The approved back keeps its prior lighting setup.

All 952 retained frame vertices match R8 in position and color. The rear-facing
vertices also match, the new relief remains in front of the bronze backing, and
the overall asset bounds are unchanged. The R8 back remains approved. The R12
front is pending user review. Exact pixel parity is not claimed: the frame's
mounts, some block placement, material finish and shading still differ.

Both current images are fresh 1254 by 1254 native Unreal Lit captures of the
saved R12 static mesh. They were visually inspected. The saved mesh has 23,504
triangles and zero saved normal errors. The Collectibles gallery was saved and
reopened to verify its mesh, material and ground contact. Its other seven asset
bindings are unchanged, and its overview was recaptured. HomesteadReference was
restored without changing project defaults. Gameplay and collision were not tested.
