# Homestead Riverbank - revision 6

The generic light green palette was replaced with dark olive ground, yellow-olive
vegetation, muted ochre cattail heads and gray stones. Ground blocks are now 6 cm
instead of the previous 12.5 cm. Main reed stems are 1.65 cm wide instead of
3.75 cm, and heads are 4.1 cm instead of 10 cm.

The old recipe explicitly added four large rectangular clumps. Those are removed.
The left bank now consists of small voxel columns forming several uneven peaks,
with tapered sides and a stepped reverse. An irregular ground apron extends in
front of and around the vegetation, with sparse short shoots and scattered stones.
Twenty narrow cattails occupy the middle and rear so the apron remains visible.

Both current views are native Unreal Lit renders of the saved R6 static mesh at
1402 by 1122, matching the original image dimensions. An orthographic 45-degree
front view and opposite reverse view expose block size, silhouette and ground
extension. Both images were inspected without painting over the render.

The saved mesh has 50,160 triangles and zero saved normal errors. The Environment
gallery was saved and reopened to verify the mesh, material and ground contact.
Its other six mesh bindings are unchanged, its overview was recaptured, and
HomesteadReference was restored without changing project defaults.

The revision is pending user review. Exact pixel parity is not claimed: individual
column heights, reed and stone positions, surface texture and lighting still
differ from the source. Gameplay and collision were not tested.
