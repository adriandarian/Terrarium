# Ember Crest - revision 8

The reverse now follows the approved Deep Delver R8 back: a broad raised bronze
rim surrounds a darker recessed center, with four small rear caps. The border
follows the Crest's stepped outline. A narrow raised outer edge catches the light.
The center face is 3.15 cm behind the main rim and 4.2 cm behind its outer edging.
The rear uses smooth bronze colors without the previous procedural grain or
metallic mask. The sides and front retain their original patinated material.
There are no grips or shield fittings.

The R7 front geometry, flame colors and material masks are preserved. Comparing
12,356 vertices forward of the changed rear confirmed identical position, color
and material-mask values. The saved R8 mesh has 24,076 triangles and zero saved
normal errors. Its red flame and gem vertices remain nonmetallic.

Both current images are fresh 1254 by 1254 native Unreal Lit renders of the saved
R8 mesh. They were visually inspected without painting or editing the images.
Camera and lighting settings are recorded with the captures. The reverse design
is pending user review; front pixel parity is not claimed.
