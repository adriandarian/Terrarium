# Grove - revision 6

The leaves now use staggered block depths on both faces. The light green front
veins are projecting cubes rather than a thin surface stripe. Darker green blocks
sit at three front depths and three reverse depths, with additional raised reverse
blocks. Each leaf retains a joined, stepped silhouette and connects to a forked stem.

The previous rectangular platform was replaced by a coarse irregular mound. Its
footprint changes across six rows of soil columns; the moss rises around the stem,
forms uneven ledges, and hangs down over selected soil blocks. The base is wider
relative to the leaves and uses larger blocks, following the source's proportions.

Both current views are fresh 1254 by 1254 native Unreal Lit captures of the saved
R6 mesh. Front and reverse were visually inspected. The mesh has 10,120 triangles
and zero saved normal errors. The Collectibles gallery was saved and reopened to
verify the mesh, material and ground contact, with all seven other mesh bindings
unchanged. Its overview was recaptured, and HomesteadReference was restored.

The new revision is pending user review. Exact pixel parity is not claimed: leaf
contours, stem shape, precise block placement and lighting still differ from the
concept. The back is an authored continuation because no source back was supplied.
Gameplay and collision were not tested. Project defaults were preserved.
