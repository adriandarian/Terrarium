# Ground ecology V5

This additive source pass replaces the visual impression of empty lawns with irregular ground-layer planting near all five settlements and along their connecting roads. It adds no trees: thickets combine low meadow tufts, ground plants, flowers, shrubs and small bushes; occasional stone clusters interrupt their edges. Broken low masonry follows selected stretches of settlement pad margins, and village side plots gain planted beds and flower borders.

The deterministic seed is 927051. `ecology-v5-layout.json` contains 20,454 planned instances in 316 accepted cluster regions. Asset counts are 12,067 grass tufts, 3,056 ground plants, 1,785 flowers, 1,355 bushes, 1,211 shrubs, 557 rock clusters, 254 low stone modules, 54 shore outcrops, 22 moss rocks, 62 garden beds and 31 wheat modules. Terrain traces can reject planned points, so the native integration receipt is authoritative for final counts.

Asset dimensions come from the retained HomesteadPilot native contracts. Grass is approximately 0.50 m wide and 0.35 m tall; ground plants are about 0.71 m across and 0.26 m tall; rock clusters are about 1.18 by 0.80 m and 0.54 m tall; bushes are approximately 1.21 by 1.04 m and 1.12 m tall. Scales stay close to these dimensions. The 4 m stone module is explicitly reduced to a roughly 2.6 m long, 0.65 m deep, 0.50 m high edging segment, rather than used as a large terrain replacement.

The plan excludes the original home and apron within an axis-aligned 106 m square, road widths plus each asset radius and 1.15 m of additional clearance, building/entrance areas, and civic squares. It preserves all previous actors and foliage types. New foliage has no collision and uses project-private `FT_WX_EcologyV5_*` assets.

Run `Scripts/WorldExpansion/ecology_v5.py` to regenerate the source layout. The coordinator runs `ecology_v5_integrate.py` in the verified ValleyRegion editor. Optional `ecology-v5-request.json` with a `groups` list selects independently rerunnable asset groups. For example, `["Bush", "Flowers", "RockCluster"]` integrates only those families. Omit the request or `groups` to process everything.

The integrator traces the current native terrain for every point, ignoring foliage. It rejects non-terrain hits, riverbed/water-level hits and steep slopes, then applies a small, recorded embedding depth so grass roots and stone bases sit in the actual current surface. It validates native mesh dimensions, source file hashes, material paths and new instance count deltas. Ground details request native distance culling at 180 m, shrubs/bushes at 350 m and larger stones/crops at 600 m; the receipt reports whether the editor accepted that setting. Actual transform, contact and component data is stored in `ecology-v5-integration.json` for reopened validation.

Completed here: source generation, deterministic exclusion checks and Python compilation. No editor calls, terrain/material edits or changes to prior source packages were performed. Native placement, appearance, persisted contacts, runtime traversal and performance remain coordinator validation.

V5 grounding reads `SourceAssets/WorldExpansion/TerrainV5/manifest.json` and accepts the exact `WX_VoxelGround_*` and `WX_VoxelRock_*` actor labels for both collidable surface roles. The measured `WX_HomeApron` remains eligible outside the protected home box. The integration receipt records its full whitelist, so old analytical terrain heights are never substituted for V5 native contacts.

`ecology_v5_path_materials.py` is a separate coordinator operation for matching existing urban paving to the generated gravel. The authored WornPath2m mesh uses three slots: path ground, light stones and darker stones. The script applies `M_VoxelPath` to slot zero only on the two region-private paving FoliageTypes, preserving the two stone shaders. Native instance transforms are captured before removing/readding those two types and checked for exact persisted-value equality afterward. Source mesh hashes, effective component material bindings and instance counts are recorded in `ecology-v5-path-materials.json`. Original home paving and shared meshes/materials remain unchanged.
