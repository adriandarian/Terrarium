# The inhabited valley

The starting homestead belongs to a settled river valley. Places should be recognizable by their plan, silhouette, approach and surrounding land use, before adding labels or quests. Warm timber and plaster, terracotta roofs, mossy stone and selective teal remain the shared architectural language.

## Five different places

- **Alderhaven:** the valley's market and civic center. Crooked merchant lanes gather around a small market, while a raised court and bell tower provide a visible destination. Gardens and orchards soften its boundary. The skyline should lead the player in from the southern road.
- **Stonegate:** an upland gate town. A bent entrance street and asymmetric enclosure contrast with Alderhaven's open market plan. Two masonry bastions mark arrival. It remains a modest fortified settlement rather than a second copy of the city.
- **Brookmere:** orchard husbandry and garden plots. Its lanes bend around a green, and enclosed fruit trees occupy meaningful parts of the village footprint.
- **Reedbank:** a trading village with a route down to the mill. The new mill precinct gives the river a human use and the village a destination beyond its center.
- **Highfield:** a compact farming community at the edge of bounded grain fields. Its open cultivated ground contrasts with Brookmere's tree canopy.

## Travel and history

The **river mill** ties the farming settlements to the water. Masonry meets the bank, a modeled wheel occupies the river face, and timber stairs connect the precinct to a lower landing. The wheel is static; production, trade simulation and water-driven animation are not implemented.

The **old aqueduct** suggests an earlier layer of infrastructure along the upland route. Its open arches and incomplete top courses are visible scenery, reached along a terrain-fitted trail. No quest, faction history or restoration mechanic is implied by this placement.

## Art boundaries

The imagegen concept is a direction reference, not a representation of the current renderer. V6 implements irregular layouts, fields, civic hierarchy, the river precinct, the upland ruin, new limestone paving and a continuous angular terrain surface. It still uses a small inherited building family; unique district architecture, more varied rooflines, interiors, character life and larger cities remain future art and gameplay work.

Preserve the detailed original homestead. Further expansion should add distinct destinations and stronger connections between them, then assess the native scene from human height as well as from regional overview cameras.
